A "Leg Raise 90" (meaning raising your legs to a 90-degree angle) is generally considered suitable for most ages and weights, as long as there are no pre-existing health conditions, but beginners, particularly those with lower back concerns, should start with a modified version and gradually progress; it's always best to consult a doctor before starting any new exercise routine, especially if you are older or have health concerns. 
Key points to consider:
Benefits for most ages:
Leg raises primarily target the core muscles, which are important for stability and posture at any age. 
Modifications for beginners:
If you're new to exercise or have back issues, start with smaller leg raises, keeping your knees slightly bent to reduce strain. 
Importance of proper form:
Maintaining a flat back and engaging your core throughout the exercise is crucial to avoid injury.
