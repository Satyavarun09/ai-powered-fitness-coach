The cat-cow pose is a gentle yoga pose that's suitable for most people, including kids, pregnant people, and men over 40. However, you should avoid it if you have certain injuries or intense back pain. 
Suitability 
Kids: Helps improve posture and combat upper body slouch
Pregnant people: Helps relieve back tension and improve circulation in the spine
Men over 40: Can help with back pain
Benefits 
Increases flexibility in the neck, shoulders, and spine
Stretches the muscles of the hips, back, abdomen, and chest
Strengthens the spine
Releases tension in the neck and upper back
Improves posture
Calms the mind
Increases blood circulation
Contraindications 
Avoid if you have a wrist, arm, shoulder, or knee injury
If you have intense back pain, avoid or practice only under the guidance of a certified yoga teacher
How to do it 
Start on your hands and knees
Inhale, arch your back, and lift your head and tailbone towards the ceiling
Exhale, round your spine, and tuck your chin towards your chest
