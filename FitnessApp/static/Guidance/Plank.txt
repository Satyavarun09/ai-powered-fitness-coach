A plank exercise is recommended for people of all ages and weight levels as it can be easily modified to suit different fitness levels, with beginners starting on their knees and progressing to a full plank on toes as they build strength; however, it's important to consult a doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have any health concerns. 
Key points about planks and different fitness levels:
Beginners: Start with a knee plank (on knees instead of toes) for shorter durations (30 seconds) and gradually increase the time as they get stronger. 
Intermediate: Perform a full plank on toes, aiming for longer holds (1 minute or more). 
Advanced: Add variations like side planks, elevated planks, or weighted planks to further challenge the core.
