The Superman pose, also known as Viparita Shalabhasana, is a yoga pose that can be done by people of all ages, genders, and fitness levels. It's a beginner to intermediate posture that can help build strength in the lower back. 
Who can do it
People of all ages, genders, and fitness levels can do the Superman pose 
It can be used to complement other core-targeting asanas such as situps and leg raises 
People with chronic back injuries should avoid this move or speak with a healthcare professional first 
It's not recommended for pregnancy 
How to do it 
Lie facedown with your arms and legs outstretched
Exhale as you slowly lift arms, legs, and head off the floor
Keep arms and legs straight (not locked) as you hold up your weight
Hold for 5 seconds
Exhale as you lower back down
Benefits 
Targets the abs, hamstrings, glutes, and lower back muscles
Tones the abs and back
Stretches the shoulders and chest
Improves digestion
