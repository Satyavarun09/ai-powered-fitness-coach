A bicycle crunch is generally considered suitable for most ages and weight levels, as long as proper form is used, but beginners or those with concerns should consult a healthcare professional before starting; it's a great exercise for building core strength and can be modified to suit different fitness levels by adjusting the number of repetitions and intensity. 
Key points about bicycle crunches and age/weight:
Beginner friendly:
Even if you are new to exercise or have a higher weight, you can start with modified bicycle crunches by keeping your feet on the ground and focusing on controlled movements. 
Adaptable:
You can adjust the difficulty by changing the speed, range of motion, and number of repetitions based on your fitness level. 
Important considerations:
Back pain: If you have pre-existing back pain, consult a professional before doing bicycle crunches to ensure proper form and avoid aggravation. 
Joint issues: People with knee or hip issues might need to modify the exercise to reduce strain. 
Who can benefit from bicycle crunches:
Most adults:
From young adults to older individuals, with appropriate modifications based on fitness level. 
Athletes:
Can incorporate bicycle crunches into their training for enhanced core stability. 
People looking to improve posture:
Strengthening the core muscles through bicycle crunches can aid in maintaining good posture.
