A LegRaise0, also known as a thighplasty, is typically recommended for people who are 18 years or older and have excess skin on their inner thighs. It can be a good option for people who have lost a significant amount of weight. 
Age 
The optimal age for a thigh lift is usually 18 years or older.
Weight
Ideal candidates are people who are close to their ideal body weight and don't experience significant weight fluctuations. 
Patients who have lost a large amount of weight and have loose sagging skin on the inner thighs may be a good fit. 
Health
Patients should not have any health issues that may get in the way of the post-surgery healing process. 
Major medical conditions such as heart and lung conditions may be a relative contraindication for this procedure.
