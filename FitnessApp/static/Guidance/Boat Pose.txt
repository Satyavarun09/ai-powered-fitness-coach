The boat pose (navasana) is an intermediate yoga pose that's suitable for many people, including children, but some people should avoid it. 
Suitability
Children: The rocking action of the boat pose can help with balance and posture. 
Adults: The boat pose is a core stabilizer that can help with balance, digestion, and weight loss. 
Precautions 
Asthma and heart patients: Should avoid this pose.
Pregnant women: Should avoid this pose.
Women during menstruation: Should avoid this pose during the first two days of their menstrual cycle.
How to do the pose 
Sit on a yoga mat with your spine straight.
Rest your hands on the ground by your hips.
Lift your feet up, keeping your knees bent.
Reach your arms forward with your palms facing each other.
Straighten your legs.
Try to keep your lower belly flat and firm.
Point your toes or flex through your heels.
Breathe.
Tips 
Try to stay in the pose for 10 to 20 seconds, slowly increasing your time to a minute.
