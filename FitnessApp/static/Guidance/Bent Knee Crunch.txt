Bent knee crunches can be recommended for people of all ages who want to lose weight and strengthen their core. 
Who can do bent knee crunches 
Age: People of all ages can do bent knee crunches
Weight: People who are overweight can do bent knee crunches to lose weight and strengthen their core
How to do bent knee crunches 
Start with a light weight, such as 5 or 10 pounds
Gradually increase the weight as your core becomes stronger
Get a comfortable grip
Use a weight that you're comfortable holding during the situp
Benefits of bent knee crunches 
Bent knee crunches can help you return to a healthy weight
They can help you lose belly fat more quickly
They can help strengthen your core
