The reverse prayer pose (Paschima Namaskar) can be practiced by children, teens, and adults, but it depends on their flexibility, strength, and any injuries they have. 
Age 
Children and teens can be introduced to the pose to improve focus and concentration
Senior citizens can practice the pose in a seated position using a chair
Strength
People who are building strength in their quadriceps, knees, and flexibility in their arms can practice warm-up poses before attempting the pose 
People with tight shoulders and upper body should warm up their upper body with stretches before attempting the pose 
Injuries
People with shoulder, wrist, ankle, elbow, and cervical injuries or surgeries should avoid the pose 
People with low blood pressure should avoid the pose
