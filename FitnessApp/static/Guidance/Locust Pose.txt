The locust pose (Shalabhasana) is generally suitable for people of most ages, but some people should avoid it. 
Suitability
Age: Most people can perform the locust pose, including children and teens 
Weight: The pose can be suitable for people of different weights 
Precautions
Back pain: People with acute or chronic back pain or recent back injuries should avoid the pose 
High blood pressure: The pose can lead to respiratory difficulties, especially for people with high blood pressure 
Pregnancy: Pregnant women should avoid the pose to prevent stress on the abdominal area and lower back 
Sciatica: People with severe sciatica should avoid the pose as it can make symptoms worse 
Weak spine: People with a weak spine should avoid the pose 
Low bone density: People with low bone density should avoid the pose 
Neck injuries: People with neck injuries should avoid the pose 
Benefits 
Increases body flexibility
Corrects posture
Strengthens back muscles
Works on the entire core
