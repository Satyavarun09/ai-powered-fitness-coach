Frog pose, also known as Mandukasana, is generally safe for most people of all ages and fitness levels. However, you should avoid it if you have certain injuries or conditions. 
Safety
Avoid this pose if you have knee, groin, or hip injuries or discomfort 
Pregnant women should avoid this pose after the first trimester 
People with high blood pressure should avoid this pose 
People who have recently had abdominal surgery should avoid this pose 
Benefits 
Improves hip flexibility
Relieves back pain
Relaxes the pelvic floor
Strengthens the core and lower back
Improves posture
Increases range of motion
Stimulates the digestive system
Reduces stress and anxiety
Enhances reproductive health
