A side plank is generally considered appropriate for most ages and weight levels, as long as proper form is maintained, but beginners or individuals with limitations should start with modified versions and consult a professional if needed; it's particularly beneficial for building core strength and stability across a wide range of ages, including older adults, with modifications as necessary based on individual fitness levels. 
Key points to remember:
Beginners:
If you're new to exercise, start with shorter holds and modified side planks on your forearms to build up strength gradually. 
Older adults:
Can benefit from side planks to maintain core stability, but should focus on proper form and shorter holds, potentially using support from their hands or knees if needed. 
Individuals with limitations:
Consult a healthcare professional or physical therapist if you have any injuries, joint pain, or other conditions that might affect your ability to perform a side plank safely.
