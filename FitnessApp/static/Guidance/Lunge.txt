Lunges are considered a suitable exercise for people of most ages and weight levels, as they can be easily modified to suit individual abilities by adjusting the intensity and adding or removing weight; however, beginners or those with joint concerns should start with bodyweight lunges and focus on proper form before progressing to heavier weights. 
Key points about lunges and age/weight:
All ages:
Lunges can be beneficial for people of all ages, including older adults, with proper form and modifications as needed. 
Beginners:
Start with bodyweight lunges to learn proper technique before adding external weight. 
Weight considerations:
Lower weight individuals: May need to add minimal weight to challenge themselves. 
Higher weight individuals: May benefit from bodyweight lunges initially or consider lighter weights to maintain proper form.
