The Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana) is generally considered suitable for most ages and body types when practiced with proper alignment and modifications, but it's especially beneficial for beginners and can be practiced by people of most ages, as long as they don't have any pre-existing back injuries, recent abdominal surgery, or pregnancy complications; always consult a doctor before starting any new exercise routine, especially if you have any health concerns. 
Key points to remember about Cobra Pose:
Good for beginners:
Cobra pose can be adapted to different levels of flexibility, making it suitable for beginners when done with a gentle back bend and proper form. 
Potential concerns:
Individuals with back injuries, recent abdominal surgery, carpal tunnel syndrome, or pregnancy should avoid the Cobra Pose or consult a qualified yoga teacher for modifications. 
Benefits:
It helps to open the chest, strengthen the back muscles, improve spinal flexibility, and can be beneficial for digestion.
