A leg raise set of 30 repetitions is generally considered appropriate for most healthy adults in their 20s to mid-30s with an average weight, as it provides a challenging yet achievable workout for a strong core; however, always consult a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you have any pre-existing medical conditions or are new to exercise. 
Key points to consider:
Beginner level: If you're new to exercise, start with a lower rep range (like 10-15) and gradually increase as your strength improves.
Intermediate level: Individuals with moderate fitness can aim for 20-30 leg raises per set.
Advanced level: More experienced individuals can attempt higher rep ranges (30+) or add weight to the exercise for added difficulty. 
Important factors to remember:
Proper form: Maintaining good form is crucial to prevent injuries; ensure your lower back is pressed against the ground and you are engaging your core throughout the movement. 
Listen to your body: If you experience pain, stop the exercise and consult a professional. 
Variation is key: Try different leg raise variations to target different abdominal muscles.
