A "Leg Raise 60" (likely referring to performing 60 repetitions of leg raises) is generally considered appropriate for individuals in their younger to mid-adult years with a relatively average weight, as it presents a moderate challenge for most people; however, always consult with a healthcare professional before starting any new exercise routine, especially if you are over 50 or have any health concerns. 
Key points to consider:
Age:
Younger adults (20s-40s) tend to have better core strength and flexibility, making 60 leg raises more manageable. As you age, it's often recommended to adjust the number of repetitions downwards to suit your fitness level.
Weight:
Individuals with a higher bodyweight may find 60 leg raises more challenging due to the increased load on their core muscles. In such cases, starting with fewer repetitions and gradually increasing them might be better.
Fitness Level:
If you are new to exercise, start with a lower number of leg raises and gradually progress to higher reps as your core strength improves.
