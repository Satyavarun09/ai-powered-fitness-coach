Pigeon pose is suitable for children ages 5 and up, as well as adults of various weights. However, you should avoid the pose if you have certain conditions. 
Age
Children ages 5 and up can do the pigeon pose 
Men over 40 can use the half pigeon stretch to loosen hips 
Weight 
The pose can be suitable for people of various weights
Conditions
You should avoid the pose if you have chronic hip, knee, or lower back issues 
Pregnant women should speak with their doctor first 
People with ankle or knee injuries should avoid the pose 
People with pelvic or groin injuries should avoid the pose 
Modifications
You can modify the pose by using a pillow or block under your hips 
You can do the pose seated in a chair
