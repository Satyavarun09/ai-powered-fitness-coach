Iliopsoas stretches can be recommended for people of all ages, including healthy adults and people with lumbar lordosis. 
Age
A study on the relationship between iliopsoas muscle weakness and stiff-knee gait included participants with an average age of 22.4 years. 
A study on the efficacy of iliopsoas stretching included participants between the ages of 18 and 60. 
Weight
A study on recreational athletes included participants with a range of weights. 
A study on iliopsoas shortness included participants with a range of body weights. 
Other considerations
Stretching should be gentle and not immediately after icing. 
Stretching should not be performed while holding your breath. 
Stretching can help reduce spasm in the iliopsoas complex. 
Strengthening muscles early in life can help reduce muscle mass loss and strength as you age.
