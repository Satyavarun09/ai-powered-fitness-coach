The Claim pose (Ustrasana) can be practiced by people of various ages, but with some precautions. 
Children 
Children aged 12 and under should practice with caution.
A qualified yoga instructor should guide them through the pose.
Parents should always be present.
Teenagers 
Teenagers aged 13–17 years old can practice two to three times per week.
They should be properly warmed up and hydrated before attempting the pose.
