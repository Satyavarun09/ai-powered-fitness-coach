As a beginner in bodybuilding focusing on leg raises, it's important to start at a manageable level while allowing your body to adapt. Here’s a general guideline for incorporating leg raises into your lower body workout:

Sets and Reps

Sets: 2 to 4 sets
Reps: 8 to 12 reps
Tips

Form Over Quantity: Focus on maintaining proper form rather than just hitting a high number of reps. This helps to engage the correct muscles and prevents injury.
Rest: Take about 30 to 60 seconds of rest between sets to allow your muscles to recover.
