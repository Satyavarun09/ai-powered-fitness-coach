Leg lifts and crunches are core exercises that are generally accessible to beginners, but can be challenging for advanced practitioners. 
Leg lifts 
Who can do them: Generally accessible to beginners, but may not be ideal for people with hip or lower back injuries
How to modify: Start with a few reps at a time, or modify the move to make it easier
Crunches 
Who can do them: Generally accessible to beginners
How to modify: Start with a few reps at a time, or modify the move to make it easier
Core exercises for older adults 
Planks: Stay in a push-up position and contract your core, arm, and shoulder muscles
Opposite arm and leg raise: Get on your hands and knees and stretch out your opposite arm and leg
