A seated side bend is considered a suitable exercise for people of most ages and weight levels, particularly those looking for a low-impact core workout, including older adults and beginners, as it can be easily modified to suit individual abilities and can be done while seated on a chair; however, it's always best to consult a healthcare professional before starting any new exercise routine, especially if you have any pre-existing health conditions. 
Key points about seated side bends:
Good for seniors:
Due to its low impact and seated position, it's a great option for older adults who might have difficulty with standing exercises. 
Targets core muscles:
Primarily works the obliques, which are important for stability and posture. 
Can be modified:
Adjust the intensity by leaning further to the side or using light weights for added resistance. 
Important considerations:
Back pain concerns:
If you have significant back pain, consult your doctor before attempting seated side bends, as improper form could exacerbate the issue.
